Privacy Policy for CivicOS

Effective date: 2030-01-01
Last updated: 2026-03-01

CivicOS values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how CivicOS handles information when you use the CivicOS mobile application and related services.

1. Overview

CivicOS is a digital identity and verified participation platform. The app includes identity onboarding features such as document scanning, MRZ capture, NFC passport reading on supported devices, and related profile and participation features.

Every user's information is processed and stored on the device. Our goal is to minimize data collection and protect sensitive information through privacy-focused design.

2. Information We Process

Depending on the features you use, CivicOS may process the following categories of information on your device:

  • Identity document information you choose to scan or provide
  • Passport MRZ data
  • NFC passport chip data from supported electronic passports
  • Face image or face-related capture data used during identity-related flows
  • App settings and preferences
  • Identity status and profile-related information generated through your use of the app

3. On-Device Processing and Storage

All identity verification data — document images, MRZ data, NFC chip data, and face images — is processed locally on the device and used solely for eligibility verification. It is not stored by CivicOS, is never transmitted to our backend, and is deleted from the device once verification completes. On supported devices, hardware-backed security mechanisms may be used, including Secure Enclave (Apple) Keystore (Android) to store app-related secrets (not the user's identity information).

One item is retained on your device after verification: the 3D face scan captured during the process. It is stored locally, encrypted, and used for a single purpose — to represent you visually in the app while you are signed in. It is not transmitted, not shared, and not used for any further identification or matching. Signing out of the app removes it.

4. Information You Provide, and What Leaves Your Device

Some information is used only on your device and never reaches our servers. Other information is sent to us so the service can function. This section explains which is which.

4.1 Processed only on your device

The following is used locally during identity verification and is not transmitted to us:

  • Identity documents you scan
  • Information extracted from those documents, including MRZ data and NFC chip data
  • The face image captured during verification

4.2 The unique verification identifier

When verification succeeds, your device derives a single value from your identity information using a keyed one-way function (HMAC) and sends only that value to us. This identifier:

  • cannot be reversed to recover your identity document, your name, or any other detail it was derived from;
  • tells us nothing about who you are, your nationality, your age, or your document type;
  • exists for one purpose only — to ensure that each verified person can hold only one verified account and can participate only once in a given poll.

Because the same person always produces the same identifier, it is a persistent pseudonymous identifier. It is linked to your activity within CivicOS, and we treat it as personal data under applicable data protection law even though it does not reveal your real-world identity.

4.3 Information you enter or send us

  • Profile or preference information you enter
  • Content you post, including polls, comments, and other contributions
  • Support requests or communications you send to us

5. Permissions

CivicOS may request access to the following device capabilities when needed for a feature:

  • Camera for document scanning, MRZ capture, face capture, and related features
  • NFC for reading compatible passport chip data
  • Location for map-based or location-aware features, where available
  • Photo Library only if a feature requires access to images you choose to provide

These permissions are requested in context, when relevant to the feature being used.

6. How We Use Information

We use information processed by the app to:

  • Provide identity onboarding features
  • Scan and review identity information
  • Support verified participation and voting-related features
  • Maintain app security, integrity, and reliability
  • Respond to support requests and troubleshoot issues

7. Data Sharing

We do not have access to your personal information.

8. Data Retention

Information stored locally on your device remains there until you delete it, remove the app, clear it through app functionality, or otherwise remove it from your device, subject to platform behavior.

Please note that some secure storage mechanisms on certain platforms may behave differently from ordinary app storage.

9. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information and reduce unauthorized access, disclosure, misuse, or loss.

Security measures may include:

  • On-device encryption
  • User-specific keys
  • Hardware-backed key storage where supported
  • Least-privilege access to device capabilities
  • Secure coding and storage practices

No method of storage, processing, or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Children’s Privacy

CivicOS is not intended for children under the age of 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law.

If you believe a child has provided personal information in violation of this policy, please contact us.

11. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to:

  • Access information about how your data is handled
  • Request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Request deletion of personal data
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent
  • Lodge a complaint with a data protection authority

12. International Users

If CivicOS is made available in multiple countries, your information may be subject to the laws of jurisdictions different from your own.

13. Third-Party Services

The app may rely on standard and well-known third-party libraries, device frameworks, and service providers to support core functionality, security, and reliability.

To the best of our knowledge and based on their documented behavior, these components do not collect personal user information for their own independent purposes through the app’s intended use. We make reasonable efforts to choose privacy-conscious technologies and to minimize unnecessary data exposure.

14. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the “Last updated” date and, where required, provide additional notice.
